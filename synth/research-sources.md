## Confirmed claims (abridged)
- Pleasurable music → endogenous striatal dopamine at chills (3-0).
- Anticipation dopamine (caudate) is anatomically distinct from peak dopamine (NAcc) (3-0).
- levodopa↑ / risperidone↓ causally move musical pleasure (3-0).
- NAcc activity predicts real willingness-to-pay for unheard music (3-0).
- Pleasure = non-linear interaction of uncertainty (entropy) and surprise (info content) (3-0).
- Inverted-U: liking peaks at intermediate predictive complexity (3-0).
- Groove = predictability × surprise; medium syncopation maximizes pleasure + urge-to-move (3-0).
- Move ≈ pleasure, r ≈ .96 (2-1).
- Earworms = common global contour + uncommon interval leaps; faster tempo stickier (3-0).
- Repetition increases earworm strength, dose-dependent (3-0).
- Listeners build statistical models of exposed styles → prediction is learned (3-0).

## Refuted (killed, 0-3)
- "Musically-elicited reward prediction errors *causally drive* musical
  pleasure." RPEs are supported for *learning* and NAcc *correlation* only.
  (de Fleurian, PNAS.) — We do not build on this.
